# Session log - 15 October 2026: Apple's HIG Foundations

Asked, across all the user's repositories: bring them in line with Apple's
Human Interface Guidelines, as the new Transient Shuffler is, with the 19
Foundations pages attached. The user chose the full set Transient Shuffler
has, and to build on the unmerged 1.1.1 wording branch
(`claude/adoring-feynman-bihxp2`) rather than from `main`, so the two do not
conflict. Recorded as [decision 0009](../decisions/0009-apples-hig-foundations.md);
the page-by-page review is [`docs/apple-hig-review.md`](../apple-hig-review.md).

Midi Suggester was done first and became the pattern for the family's other
ReaImGui windows (Midi Variator, Starting Blocks and its Notation, Midi
Catalogue, Good Idea, DAW Battle Pass).

## What changed (1.2)

- Text 7:1: `DIM` `#8A919C` -> `#B0B6C0`, `WARN` `#D2483F` -> `#FF9E96`.
- View menu, Text Size 100/125/150/200%, Cmd+= / Cmd+- / Cmd+0, saved.
- `pushSize()`: the font, FramePadding (8, 7.5) for 28-point controls,
  ItemSpacing 12. `px()` on every fixed width and gap; `fit()` keeps a button
  at least as wide as its label; `chooser()` wraps at the window's edge (a row
  of 18 roots no longer fits on one line at 12 points apart).
- Keyboard navigation and its ring.
- The ink bar on chosen buttons.
- The message moved to the top with *Dismiss*.
